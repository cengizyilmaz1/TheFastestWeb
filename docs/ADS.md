# Advertisement reservation operations

The current offer is the original $19/month Dodo sidebar advertisement. Fixed-duration one-time ad products remain supported by the backend. Migration and startup never create provider products. Existing advertisements and valid entitlements retain their recorded expiry and ownership. See [Dodo subscription rules](DODO-PAYMENTS.md#advertising-inventory-and-renewals) for the current monthly package.

Migration `0005` adds `ad_inventory` and `ad_reservations`. Inventory capacity is seeded only from distinct existing sidebar coordinates. Operators can explicitly add/enable placements through the audited admin form. A product requires an owned site and a verified provider product binding. Monthly products use paid subscription periods; one-time products require a positive fixed number of publication days. Both payment integration and inventory must be enabled before checkout is available.

Availability excludes unexpired active legacy ads and every held/paid/active reservation. Row locking and the partial unique live-inventory index prevent concurrent purchases overselling one placement. At most one unpaid hold per buyer prevents a single account reserving the entire inventory. Checkout creation and the hold share a database transaction; the provider is called only after commit.

Held and paid reservations have **no automatic timeout**. Dodo's installed checkout API does not expose a supported session cancellation/expiry contract. Releasing a still-payable hosted session could charge a buyer after its placement was sold to somebody else. Definite creation rejection releases the hold; an ambiguous network error or interrupted create retains it. An ordinary failed payment attempt does not prove the hosted checkout can never be paid and does not release capacity.

After signed authoritative payment confirmation, the system records `paid`, creates inactive `pending` creative from the owned site's name, tagline/description and URL, and retains a financial record. The administrator reviews the creative in a signed preview. Confirmation rechecks payment, ownership and inventory and records its audit. A one-time placement receives its full purchased duration from approval. A monthly placement requires a verified successful current-period payment and active subscription entitlement; display ends at the provider's paid period end. Later approval does not create another free month. Paid renewals can extend approved creative; an unpaid subscription event cannot. Disputes suppress display without resetting its purchased window.

Expired one-time **active** publication windows are cleaned up automatically. Monthly reservations remain occupied while a subscription can recover or renew, including `on_hold`; terminal provider states and verified refunds follow the subscription reconciliation rules. The sidebar independently filters status/activity/expiry. Financial and placement history remain intact.

To release an unpaid held checkout, first establish in the provider account that its hosted session has been definitively cancelled and cannot charge. Record the provider cancellation reference in the admin **Release unpaid hold** action; do not include secrets or customer details. Preview and confirm it. The action rejects paid reservations or any existing succeeded payment. If a later payment contradicts recorded cancellation evidence, the event stops for manual reconciliation rather than granting an already-released placement. Investigate and resolve the provider payment before further action.

The focused administrator panel and protected backend reports expose reservation and creative state; the former owner dashboard UI has been removed. Never tell a buyer that a pending checkout is expired merely because it is old. Queue and audit IDs provide the investigation trail. Ad copy must be approved; an entitlement alone does not publish creative. Payment delivery remains disabled in demo mode.

PostgreSQL tests cover concurrent buyers, old ambiguous holds, rejected creation release, preserved legacy occupancy, pending creative, audited approval with a full publication window, duplicate payment delivery preserving expiry, verified refund release, explicit unpaid cancellation evidence, rejection of paid-hold release, ended publication windows, one unpaid hold per buyer and a late payment conflicting with a cancelled reservation. Route tests cover active-placement checks, click deduplication, origin rejection and one separately classified analytics observation per persisted click.
